Home should
feel like a verb.

We’re good at getting home. We’re not so good at arriving.

MORI began with a small observation: the rituals around coming home have disappeared. Keys down. Shoes off. Exhale. The brief was to design a tactile, low-tech object that gives those few minutes back to us.
